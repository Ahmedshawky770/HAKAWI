# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**Q: Is Hakawi free to use?**

A: Reading stories is free. Books may be purchased or rented. Contest entry
is free for writers.

**Q: Which languages does Hakawi support?**

A: The interface is currently in Arabic. Story content can be in any language.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**

A: Account deletion is handled by the support team. Contact us through the
report form with the subject "Account deletion".

## Writers

**Q: How do I publish a story?**

A: Go to **Stories → Create**, write your story, pick a category and tags,
and click **Publish**. Your story is immediately available to readers.

**Q: Can I edit a story after publishing?**

A: Yes. Open the story and choose **Edit**. Changes are saved as a new
version; the published slug does not change.

**Q: How do I submit to a contest?**

A: Open the contest page and enter the ID of one of your published stories.
You can submit one story per contest.

## Readers

**Q: How do I buy a book?**

A: Open the book page, enter your payment method ID, and click **Buy**. You
will be redirected to the payment gateway. Once confirmed, the book appears
in your library.

**Q: How long does a rental last?**

A: Rentals are available for 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, or 90 days. You choose the
length at checkout.

**Q: Can I return a book early?**

A: Yes. Open the rental detail page and click **Return**. No refund is issued
for unused days.

## Publishers

**Q: How do I create a contest?**

A: Go to **Contests → Create**, fill in the title, description, dates, and
submission deadline, and publish. The contest appears in the public list.

**Q: How do I select a winner?**

A: Open the contest page once voting has ended. Enter the winning submission
ID and click **Select winner**.

**Q: How do I distribute a prize?**

A: From the contest page, choose the winning submission, enter the prize
amount and type, and click **Distribute prize**.

## Moderation

**Q: Why was my content removed?**

A: A moderator removed it because it violated the community guidelines. You
can see the reason on your Restrictions page.

**Q: How do I appeal a moderation decision?**

A: Submit a report with the subject "Appeal" and explain why you believe the
decision was incorrect. The moderation team will review it.

## Technical

**Q: What browsers does Hakawi support?**

A: Hakawi works on the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**

A: Not yet. The web client is designed to work well on mobile browsers.

**Q: How do I report a bug?**

A: Use the report form and select the appropriate target type. Include
screenshots and the steps to reproduce the issue.
