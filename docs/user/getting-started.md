# Getting Started with Hakawi

## Welcome

Hakawi (حكاوي) is a platform for writers, readers, and publishers. This guide
will help you get up and running in a few minutes.

## Account types

Hakawi supports five account types:

- **Reader** — browse stories, buy and rent books, participate in contests.
- **Writer** — publish stories, submit to contests, build an audience.
- **Rising Star** — emerging writers with additional promotional tools.
- **Professional** — established authors with advanced analytics.
- **Publisher** — manage books, run contests, and distribute prizes.

## Creating an account

1. Open the sign-up page.
2. Enter your email, name, and preferred username.
3. Choose a password and confirm it.
4. Select your account type.

You will receive a verification email. Click the link inside to activate your
account.

## First story

1. Go to **Stories → Create**.
2. Write a title and body.
3. Pick a category and add tags.
4. Save as draft or publish immediately.

## Buying a book

1. Browse the **Books** catalogue.
2. Open a book you like.
3. Enter your saved payment method ID.
4. Choose **Buy** or **Rent**.
5. You will be redirected to the payment gateway.
6. Once the payment is confirmed, the book appears in your **Library**.

## Renting a book

Renting gives you temporary access to a book. Choose a rental length, pay the
rental fee, and the book is available for the selected period. You can extend
the rental before it expires by paying for additional days.

## Participating in a contest

1. Open an active contest.
2. Submit one of your published stories.
3. Wait for the submission deadline.
4. Vote for your favourite submissions once voting opens.
5. The winner is announced when the contest ends.

## Earning badges

Badges are awarded automatically when you reach certain milestones:

- Published your first story.
- Received your 100th reaction.
- Won a contest.
- Reached 1000 followers.

View the full badge catalogue from the **Badges** page.
