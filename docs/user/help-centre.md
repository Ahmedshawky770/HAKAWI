# Help Centre

## How do I reset my password?

Use the **Forgot password** link on the login page. You will receive an email
with a reset link. The link expires after a short period for security reasons.

## Why can't I access a book I just bought?

Access is granted only after the payment webhook confirms the transaction.
This usually takes a few seconds. If the book does not appear in your library
after two minutes, contact support.

## How do I rent a book?

Open the book page, choose a rental length, enter your payment method ID, and
click **Rent**. The rental starts as soon as the payment is confirmed. You can
read the book from your library while the rental is active.

## Can I extend a rental?

Yes. Open the rental detail page before it expires and choose **Extend**.
The cost of the extension is shown before any money moves. The maximum number
of extensions per rental is three.

## Why was my story archived?

Stories that violate the community guidelines may be archived by a moderator.
You can see the moderation action in your **Restrictions** page, which lists
any active limits on your account.

## How do I report inappropriate content?

Open the content you want to report and choose **Report**. Fill in the reason
and an optional description. The moderation team reviews every report.

## Why did my vote not count?

Votes are refused when:
- The contest is not in voting status.
- The submission has been rejected.
- You have already voted for this submission.
- You are voting for your own submission.

## Who can see my votes?

Only the aggregate vote count is public. Voter identities are never exposed
to other users.

## How are prizes distributed?

The contest owner distributes prizes from the publisher dashboard. The owner
selects the winning submission, chooses a prize type, and records the amount.
Prize distributions are visible to the contest participants.

## What happens if I am banned?

A ban prevents you from interacting with the platform. You can view the
reason and duration on your **Restrictions** page. If you believe the ban
was issued in error, contact the moderation team through the report form.
